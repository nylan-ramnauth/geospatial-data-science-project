# Pixel Closeups

This section keeps selected pixel-level QA material for VJ146A2 settlement events.

## Contents

- `scripts/`: repo-local R helpers for selected settlement closeups, candidate screens, and event-table summaries.
- `notebooks/`: stripped notebooks that can regenerate or inspect selected closeup outputs.
- `data/`: small candidate tables, KMLs, and pixel-accounting extracts.
- `reports/`: closeup screening and pixel-accounting notes.

Bulk rendered closeups are generated under `validation/closeups/figures/generated/` and are intentionally ignored. They can be regenerated from the notebooks or R helpers when external rasters and settlement panels are present.
